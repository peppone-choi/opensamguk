**DECISION = APPEND (not remap).** opensamguk levels 8 and 9 are TWO NEW tiers appended ABOVE 황제(7); levels 0..7 keep their legacy names, thresholds, history templates, income multiplier, `getNationChiefLevel` mapping, and gold/rice grant **byte-identically** (so the legacy 8-entry array remains the parity oracle for income/supply/diplomacy numerics at levels 0..7). (Unit 4 port_notes: "gold/rice grant continuation (lv*1000 extends naturally)".)
- The level-up gate is `month/1000 true` monotonic with a city-count threshold table (`nationLevelByCityCnt`, `UpdateNationLevel.php:41-50`); appending two rows above lv7 leaves all existing thresholds intact. The 0-9 variant table (A3 Task NL1) is the single source-of-truth const, with 8/9 thresholds + names + history templates DEFINED by this plan (legacy has none for >7).
- The P3 gate tests the NEW table + the 8/9 transitions (research Unit 11 / consolidated OQ #3), NOT a byte-match against the legacy 8-entry `nationLevelByCityCnt`; levels 0..7 ARE byte-gated against legacy.
