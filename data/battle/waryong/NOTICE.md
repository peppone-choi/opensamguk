# Waryong battle catalog notice

`catalog-v1.json` is an exact-byte export of the derived terrain catalog from
`peppone-choi/opensamguk-images` at merge commit
`9c890e453bb6036bc674ddfb93f2ee1e4f1efb60` (`waryong/battle/catalog-v1.json`).
It is 954,038 bytes with SHA-256
`2eb021038ccf36178127247d25c18f03f538e6f139a2e699fdb40e5ed5f4bb27`.

The catalog derives from the original game **제갈공명 와룡전**. Its use in
OpenSamguk is classified `owner-accepted`; it is outside this repository's MIT
license. That classification records the repository owner's product decision.
It grants no rights to third parties and does not claim ownership of the
original game art or map data.

The original `BATTLE.MAP`, `BATTLE.MDL`, and `BATTLE.SCH`, the conversion tool,
and source PNGs are not included in this server export. The source repository's
`waryong/LICENSE-NOTICE.md` and `.license-boundaries.json` describe its
original-game derivative boundary.
