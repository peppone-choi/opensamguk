# Waryong battle board kit notice

These files are an exact-byte export of `waryong/battle/kit/` from `peppone-choi/opensamguk-images`
at merge commit `3ef1ecd82b360d51e652aef558746c206f4d9725` (kit id `3ef1ecd`); hashes are in `export.json` next to this file.

The kit derives from the original game **제갈공명 와룡전** (`BATTLE.MAP`, `BATTLE.MDL`, `BATTLE.SCH`,
`GAMEPAL.BRG`): original pieces, record tables, board layouts, unit pieces and unit role layers. The app
assembles the boards itself (`web/shared/src/battle/battleBoard.ts`). Its use in OpenSamguk is classified
`owner-accepted` (owner decisions 2026-09-26 and 2026-09-30); it is outside this repository's MIT license,
grants no rights to third parties and does not claim ownership of the original art. The original files,
the conversion tool and pre-rendered boards are not exported here.
